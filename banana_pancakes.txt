Ingredients:

2 ripe bananas
2 large eggs
120 g all-purpose flour
240 ml oat milk
1 tsp baking powder
1 tsp vanilla extract
1 tbsp sugar
Pinch of salt
Butter

Preparation:

Mash bananas and mix with eggs, milk, and vanilla in a mixing bowl. For best results you can also use a blender to make sure the bananas are 100% smooth. Then combine flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt in a separate bowl.
Mix the dry ingredients into wet ingredients until smooth.
Heat a greased skillet over medium heat. Spoon in desired size of pancake and cook for about two minutes on both sides or until golden brown.
