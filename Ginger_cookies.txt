Ginger cookies
 
Ingredients:
¾ cup shortening
1 cup sugar plus extra for rolling
¼ cup molasses
½ cup almond flour
½ cup coconut flour
½ cup fava or garbanzo been flour
½ cup potato flour
1 egg
2tsp baking soda
1tsp salt
1tsp cinnamon
1tsp ginger
1tsp clove
½ cup desired nuts (pecans or walnuts are good)
 
Pre heat oven to 375 degrees Fahrenheit. Grease a cookie sheet with choice of oil (coconut, shortening, butter). Fill a small candy dish with extra sugar and set aside. Combine all ingredients in a bowl except the nuts and mix well with hands. Then mush in nuts. Roll into balls sized somewhere between a quarter and a golf ball, then roll the balls into the sugar to coat them in a nice even layer. Place them all on the cookie sheet and bake for about 12 minutes. This makes a fair amount so you may have to cook them in batches.
