## About the ramen

My ramen recipe is put together from many ramen dishes I have eaten at restaurants on my travels. That is why there is one rule for me: **in every city I visit, I have to go to the best-rated ramen restaurant.**

Ramen restaurants I have visited so far:

- [SORI Ramen](https://maps.app.goo.gl/uSwYZrVd2DsNNd9L7) – Mainz
- [Umami Ramen](https://maps.app.goo.gl/bc2qntvScd793UKYA) – Freiburg
- [MANYA Sushi & Sake Izakaya](https://maps.app.goo.gl/aUCmZ28WNvPtLNnn6) – Brno
