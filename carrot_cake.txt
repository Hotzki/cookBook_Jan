Ingredients:

240 g all-purpose flour
200 g grated carrots
250 g sugar
240 ml vegetable oil
4 large eggs
1 tsp baking powder
1 tsp baking soda
1 tsp ground cinnamon
0.5 tsp ground nutmeg
0.5 tsp ground clove
0.5 tsp salt
2 tsp vanilla sugar

Preparation:

Beat eggs and sugar together in a large mixing bowl. Then add the oil and vanilla.
Combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt in a separate bowl.
Then combine the wet and dry ingredients and lastly fold in the grated carrots.
Transfer batter to a large, greased baking dish and bake in the oven at 175°C for 35-40 minutes.
